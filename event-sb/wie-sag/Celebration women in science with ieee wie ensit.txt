Event date : 19/02/2024

In celebration of the International Day of Women and Girls in Science, we were invited to Lycée Gustave Flaubert by Interact Club Marsa Mind and HeForShe Cailloux to organize an exhilirating meet up to ignite young minds.From introducing IEEE and our student branch's dynamic activities to showcasing the impactful goals of our WIE Affinity Group, it was an inspiring journey. By highlighting engineering careers at ENSIT and diverse fields within, we paved pathways for future STEM leaders. Proudly part of the STAR Program, this interactive session was a fusion of learning and laughter, fueling passion for innovation!
