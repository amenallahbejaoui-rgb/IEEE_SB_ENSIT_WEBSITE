Celebrating Women in Science with IEEE ENSIT WIE SAG

Date: 19/02/2024

The "Celebrating Women in Science" event was organized by the IEEE ENSIT WIE SAG (Women in Engineering Special Affinity Group) to honor and celebrate the incredible contributions of women in STEM fields. This inspiring day brought together students, professionals, and guests to recognize the remarkable achievements of women in science and technology. Through engaging discussions, inspiring talks, and networking opportunities, the event empowered attendees and highlighted the importance of gender diversity in engineering and scientific disciplines. It served as a platform to encourage more young women to pursue their passions in STEM and showcased the positive impact women continue to make in shaping our technological future.
