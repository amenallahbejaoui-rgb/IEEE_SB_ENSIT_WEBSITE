IEEE Connect

Date: 11/09/2024

The IEEE Connect event on September 11, 2024, was an exciting opportunity to introduce new students to the IEEE ENSIT Student Branch and its subunits. We shared the many benefits of joining the IEEE community and engaged participants with a fun, interactive quiz. Special congratulations to our four quiz winners who walked away with some fantastic gifts! The event was filled with energy, learning, and fun, helping students feel connected to our innovative community.
