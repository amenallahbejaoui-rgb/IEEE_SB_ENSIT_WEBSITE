Participation in Orientational Forum ENIT

Date: 20/07/2024

Our participation in the Orientation Forum at ENIT has been truly rewarding! Together with IEEE ENSIT SB, we engaged with students, shared insights about our activities, and encouraged them to get involved with IEEE and explore the world of engineering and technology. It was inspiring to connect with so many enthusiastic students and introduce them to the opportunities IEEE offers. Grateful for the experience and excited to welcome new members into our community!
