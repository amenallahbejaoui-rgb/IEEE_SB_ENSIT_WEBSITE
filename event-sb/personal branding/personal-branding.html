Personal Branding

Date: 02/10/2024

The Personal Branding Event hosted by the IEEE ENSIT Student Branch (SB) was an enriching session designed to help students understand and build their professional identity. Held at the Higher National School of Engineers of Tunis (ENSIT), the event focused on equipping attendees with the tools and knowledge needed to craft a compelling personal brand that reflects their skills, values, and career goals.
