General Assembly

Date: 25/09/2024

Our General Assembly event was a resounding success, bringing together IEEE ENSIT SB members, administrative board members, and chapters for a comprehensive gathering. The agenda included the presentation of the 2023/2024 final report, the 2024/2025 action plan, and the budget, followed by open discussions. This event provided an invaluable platform for reflection on past achievements and planning for the future. Members engaged in insightful conversations, shaping the direction of our branch for the coming year.
