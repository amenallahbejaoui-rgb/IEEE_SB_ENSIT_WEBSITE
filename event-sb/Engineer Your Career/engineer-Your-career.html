Engineer Your Career

Date: 18/07/2024 - 19/07/2024

Engineer Your Career event, organized by IEEE ENSIT SB, IEEE ENET'Com SB, IEEE ENIS SB, and IEEE ENIB SB, was a resounding success, attracting a large number of enthusiastic new engineering students eager to explore various career paths and find their place in the engineering world. The event provided a valuable platform for students still deciding on their career orientation, offering them insights and guidance from industry experts and academics.
