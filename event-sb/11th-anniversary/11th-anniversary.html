The 11th anniversary of IEEE ENSIT SB

Date : 09/04/2024

The 11th anniversary of IEEE ENSIT SB was a momentous celebration of a decade filled with innovation and excellence. This past event brought together current and former members to reflect on the organization's journey and honor its achievements. Attendees shared inspiring stories and experiences, fostering a sense of camaraderie. The anniversary event served as a reminder of the strong foundation built by dedicated members and sparked excitement for future endeavors. We express our gratitude to all members for their contributions and eagerly anticipate many more successful years ahead.
