IEEE ENSIT SB Integration Day

Date: 04/09/2024

We are thrilled to announce the IEEE ENSIT SB Integration Day held on September 4th, 2024! This event was an incredible opportunity for newly integrated students to connect with current IEEE members, learn about our organization, and get involved in our exciting projects and initiatives. From informative presentations to engaging networking sessions, the day was filled with opportunities for personal and professional growth. Thank you to everyone who participated and made this event such a success! We are excited to welcome our new members and look forward to all the amazing things we will accomplish together.
