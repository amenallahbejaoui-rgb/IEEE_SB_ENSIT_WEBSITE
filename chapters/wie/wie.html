IEEE Women in Engineering (WIE) is the global IEEE network dedicated to promoting, recruiting, and retaining women in engineering and STEM. Participation is open to people of all genders and backgrounds who support a more inclusive technical community.

At ENSIT, WIE brings students together through mentoring, learning, outreach, and professional connections, helping turn ambition into action.

Global network, local communities

WIE in
Tunisia.

In Tunisia, WIE activities take place through the IEEE Tunisia Section and university student groups. Examples include IEEE WIE Sup'Com and the WIE ACT congress organized by ENICarthage.

For current events and ways to participate, check the groups' official channels or get in touch with WIE ENSIT.
