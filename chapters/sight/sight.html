IEEE SIGHT is a global network of IEEE volunteers who partner with underserved communities and local organizations to use technology for sustainable development.

Projects respond to locally identified needs in areas such as healthcare, education, agriculture, clean water, and disaster response. This community-led work supports the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

Key overview

Ideas that
serve people.

01

Community mission

Apply engineering to local challenges in health, education, agriculture, clean water, and disaster response, aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

02

Local SIGHT Groups

Groups include at least six IEEE members, may welcome other volunteers, and are affiliated with an IEEE Section or Student Branch.

03

Project support

IEEE SIGHT offers funding opportunities, training, educational resources, and guidance for humanitarian technology projects.

04

SIGHT Day

Celebrated each April 28, SIGHT Day marks the submission of the first SIGHT group petition.

Local action.
Global support.

The IEEE SIGHT portal shares membership information, funding opportunities, training, news, and guidance for starting or joining a group.

In Tunisia, IEEE Tunisia Section connects local SIGHT groups and hosts the annual SIGHT Day Congress. IEEE-ENSIT-SIGHT-GROUP ENSIT is one example of a student branch group working locally.
