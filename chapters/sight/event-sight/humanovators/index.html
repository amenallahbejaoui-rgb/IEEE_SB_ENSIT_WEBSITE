About Us

What is Humanovators?

Humanovators is an event dedicated to entrepreneurship for humanity, organized by IEEE ENSIT SIGHT and the IEEE ENSIT Student Branch.

This event will focus on water and its challenges, bringing together social startups and innovators to present sustainable solutions to water management issues.
