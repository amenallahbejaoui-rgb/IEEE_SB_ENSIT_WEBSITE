Ocean Pioneers:

Event date: 11 Apr 2025

Time: 02:00 PM to 04:00 PM

Locatisation: Mostapha Khasnadar High School, Denden, Bardo

Attendees: 21

In celebration of Education Week 2025, IEEE ENSIT SIGHT GROUP proudly organized "OCEAN PIONEERS", an interactive and educational workshop held at College Khaznadar. This dynamic event aimed to ignite curiosity and raise awareness among young students about the wonders of marine life and the urgent need to protect our oceans. To inspire action and innovation, we discussed how technology and robotics are becoming powerful allies in the fight to save our oceans. From underwater drones that monitor marine life to AI systems detecting illegal fishing patterns, the students were introduced to cutting-edge solutions and encouraged to dream big. Learning was anything but boring! The workshop concluded with Ocean Quiz, a fun-filled challenge testing students? knowledge of marine facts, ocean conservation, and sea creatures.
