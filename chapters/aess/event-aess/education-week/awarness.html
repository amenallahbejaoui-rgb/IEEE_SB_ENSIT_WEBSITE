Making STEM feel real

As part of Education Week, IEEE AESS ENSIT, in collaboration with INJAZ Tunisia, visited College Pilote Ali Trad to introduce students to the fascinating world of drones.

Through interactive discussions and demonstrations, students explored how technology, creativity, and innovation can shape the future. It was a memorable experience that sparked curiosity, encouraged learning, and inspired future engineers and innovators.
