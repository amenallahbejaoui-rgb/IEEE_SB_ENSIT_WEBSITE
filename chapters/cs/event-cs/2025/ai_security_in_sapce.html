AI & CYBERSECURITY in SPACE

An online session on cybersecurity was held with Oussama Ben Hadj Dahman, Co-founder and Cybersecurity Lead at IMEDRA SPHERE. The workshop focused on introducing over 30 of our members to the fundamentals of cybersecurity and its real-world applications. Mr. Ben Hadj Dahman, a certified professional holding credentials such as ISO 27001 Lead Implementer, eJPT, CPT, and CDFE, shared his experience as a researcher and entrepreneur, offering both strategic and technical perspectives on developing effective security solutions.
