Around 20 curious members / IEEE CS ENSIT

Connect, learn, and grow together.

CS TA7KI 2.0 brought around 20 new members together for a casual coffee talk. Participants were introduced to IEEE and the Computer Society, explored the chapter’s achievements and upcoming initiatives, and enjoyed an open exchange of ideas. Fedi Bahloul shared his experience as a former Chair, while Mouhanned Jawadi and Mohamed El Abed offered insights into IT careers. Molka Weslati also presented the chapter’s mission and activities.
