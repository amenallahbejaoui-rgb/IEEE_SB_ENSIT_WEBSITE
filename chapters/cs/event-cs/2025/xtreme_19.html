25 October / IEEE ENSIT × IEEE SUPCOM

One night. Infinite possibilities.

On October 25th, IEEE ENSIT SB, in collaboration with IEEE SUPCOM SB, hosted IEEEXtreme 19.0, the annual 24-hour global programming competition. Participants from both branches joined forces in a night filled with coding, collaboration, and creativity. Our teams ranked among the top 30 in Tunisia, while networking, teamwork, and a surprise encrypted challenge made the event a true community experience.
