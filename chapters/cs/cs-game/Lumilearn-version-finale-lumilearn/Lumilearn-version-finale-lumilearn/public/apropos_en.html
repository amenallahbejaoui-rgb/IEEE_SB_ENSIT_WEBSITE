Our History 📖

During the Tech4Good Lebanon × Tunisia Challenge, the IEEE SIGHT Group ENSIT was honored as a Tunisia Pitch Finalist for pitching LumiLearn, a solar-powered, offline digital learning hub designed for underserved school in Tunisia.

Following this achievement, under the leadership of Baha Eddine Hammou, IEEE SIGHT ENSIT submitted a funding application to Tech4Good and successfully secured support, turning the concept into a real and deployable solution.

Real Implementation 🛠️

LumiLearn was brought to life through an on-site implementation at Sidi Moualla Primary School, Siliana, Tunisia, where the team installed a Raspberry Pi 5, powered by a solar setup configured so students could access the platform without internet.

During the deployment, children and teachers tested games, quizzes, books, and audio lessons directly from provided tablets.
