About Us

Engineers building tomorrow, together.

ENSIT Student Branch was founded in 2013 and has been active ever since. Today it gathers over 120 members, growing through IEEE's causes and values.

Our mission is to foster technological innovation for the benefit of humanity, while strengthening soft skills, technical knowledge, and leadership.
